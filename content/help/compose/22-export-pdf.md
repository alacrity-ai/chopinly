**File ▾ → Export PDF** opens the export sheet. **File ▾ → Save to Scores as PDF** does the same thing with the library as its target.

![The export sheet, with a live preview of page 1.](/img/help/export-sheet.png)

## It is a real PDF

Vector outlines, not a picture of a page. The notes are drawn from Bravura's own outlines and the words are embedded font subsets, so it prints at any size and a reader can select the title. There is no raster fallback anywhere.

## The controls

- **Layout** — opens [the Layout editor](help:layout), where you arrange bars into rows on the paper itself. The hint beside it reads *automatic*, or how many pins you have set.
- **size** — the staff size, from 1.4 to 2.5 mm a staff space, in small steps. The readout shows the staff height and the page count as you go.
- **page** — Letter or A4.
- **margins** — narrow, normal or wide.
- **header** — the title and composer on page 1, and a running head with the page number after it.

## The preview is the page

The preview is the **same engraver** drawing the **same plan** the PDF is built from — not an approximation. The systems on preview page 2 are the systems on PDF page 2, and the preview does not clip at the margin where the PDF would not, so a tempo word above the first staff or a pedal line under the last is visible rather than quietly hidden.

When the piece runs past one page a **pager** appears under the paper — *‹ page 1 of 3 ›* — with the running head as the PDF sets it.

A system is never split across a page, page 1 keeps room for the title block, and every page keeps enough air that nothing prints into a margin. Trailing empty bars are trimmed first; an empty piece prints its eight bars of manuscript.

## Getting the file

**Save PDF** offers **Save to device** — straight into Downloads or Files — or **Share…**, where a share sheet exists. Both are there because the share sheet alone does not save.

## Add to Scores

**Add to Scores** puts the PDF into your [Scores](app:scores) library in one tap, with the title, composer and tags it already has, a thumbnail and rendered pages like any other upload.

The composition **remembers the score it made**. Send it again after changing the music and it **replaces that score's file in place**: the pages re-render, the thumbnail is remade, and your bookmarks, your Pencil ink and the goal it is linked to all stay. You can annotate your own engraving and keep revising the engraving.

Send unchanged bytes and nothing is duplicated — the same score reopens.
