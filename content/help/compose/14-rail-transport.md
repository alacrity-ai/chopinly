## Playing

**Play** starts from the playhead at the piece's tempo; the button becomes pause. **Stop** returns to the start. `Space` plays and pauses, `Home` stops.

A playhead line follows the music, and the view scrolls only when the playing system leaves the screen — it will not yank the page out from under you while you are writing.

You can edit while it plays; the sequence picks up your change from the current position.

## Seeking

The two arrows step a bar back and a bar forward. The slider scrubs, and reads *bar N of M*.

## What playback follows

More than the notes. Repeats, endings and the jumps unroll into passes; *D.S. al Coda* goes where it says. Tempo marks change the speed as they arrive. The pedal holds notes until it lifts. Grace notes, tremolo, trills, mordents and turns are played, fermatas are held, notes under a slur play legato, and ties merge into one sounding note.

Short bars play short. What you hear is the form as written.

## Tempo

`♩=` with `−` and `+`; hold either to run. Tap the number to type one. Twenty to three hundred.

The tempo is **saved with the piece**, so it travels to your other devices and into MusicXML. It is not an undoable edit.

To put a *written* tempo mark on a bar — *Allegro*, `♩ = 120`, a change partway through — use the tempo mark on [the Form rail](help:rail-form). This rail is the speed you audition at; that one is what the paper says.
