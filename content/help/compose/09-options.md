**Options ▾** at the right of the header is Compose's control panel. It has three captioned sections, and it **stays open while you flip things**, so you can turn on two rails and a gesture in one visit.

![The Options panel: Input, Rails and Help.](/img/help/options-panel.png)

## Input

- **Pen ∣ Touch** — which contacts the score listens to. See [Pen and Touch](help:input). It is absent on a device with no touch screen.
- **Gesture** — lasso, strike and the chevrons. Off by default. See [Gesture mode](help:gestures).
- **Favorites** — show or hide the floating palette. See [the Favorites palette](help:favorites).

The rows keep their quiet ink even when they are on; the pill at the right carries the state. The accent is reserved for what the *next tap* does.

## Rails

A checklist of the eight rails. Three are on to start with — Controls, Transport and Notes — and the other five are one tick away:

| rail | what is on it |
|---|---|
| [Controls](help:rail-controls) | undo, redo, Select ∣ Pan, the clipboard, zoom |
| [Transport](help:rail-transport) | play, seek, tempo |
| [Notes](help:rail-notes) | voices, durations, dot, tie, tuplet, accidentals |
| [Key · time · clef · marks](help:rail-keys) | signatures, clefs, articulations, ornaments, slurs |
| [Dynamics · hairpins · text](help:rail-dynamics) | everything that sits on a beat |
| [Form](help:rail-form) | barlines, repeats, endings, jumps, tempo marks, bars |
| [Piano](help:rail-piano) | pedal, octave lines, fingering |
| [Grace · tremolo · marks](help:rail-marks) | grace notes, tremolo, trills, engraving overrides |

Every rail you show costs the score a lane of height, so most people keep two or three out and turn the others on when they need them. Your choice is remembered per device.

## Help

One row, which opens this help. It is also on the compositions list as a `?`, and `?` on a keyboard opens it from the editor.
