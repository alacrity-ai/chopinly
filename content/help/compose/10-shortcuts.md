`⌘` on a Mac or an iPad with a keyboard, `Ctrl` elsewhere.

## Values

| key | does |
|---|---|
| `1` – `7` | 64th, 32nd, sixteenth, eighth, quarter, half, whole |
| `.` | dot — cycles none · dotted · double dotted |
| `t` | tie |

With a selection these retype it; with nothing selected they arm. That is [the one rule](help:rails) every button follows.

## Moving and choosing

| key | does |
|---|---|
| `←` `→` | the previous / next thing in the staff |
| `Shift` `←` `→` | extend the selection |
| `↑` `↓` | re-pitch by a staff step — or nudge a rest, or move a mark away from the staff |
| `←` `→` on a mark | slide it a slot earlier or later |
| `⌘/Ctrl` `⇧` `↑` `↓` | send the note to the other staff (cross-staff) |
| `Delete` `Backspace` | delete — a rest takes its place |

## Modes

| key | does |
|---|---|
| `v` | Select |
| `h` | Pan |
| `Esc` | step back out: a listening favorite slot, an armed cursor, a paste, a selection, then Select ⇄ Place |

## Clipboard and history

| key | does |
|---|---|
| `⌘/Ctrl` `C` `X` `V` | copy, cut, paste — paste arms a cursor, then tap where it goes |
| `⌘/Ctrl` `Z` | undo |
| `⌘/Ctrl` `⇧` `Z` | redo |

## Voices and playback

| key | does |
|---|---|
| `⌘/Ctrl` `1` – `4` | the active voice |
| `Space` | play / pause |
| `Home` | stop |

## Help

| key | does |
|---|---|
| `?` | open this help |
