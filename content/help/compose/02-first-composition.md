## 1 · Make one

Open Compose and tap **new**. Give the piece a title and, if you like, a composer and tags — the same details a score in your library carries. Tap **start composing** and a blank piano score opens: treble and bass, C major, 4/4, eight empty bars, and the **quarter already armed**.

![The compositions list. Tap new to start; hold a row for its details.](/img/help/compositions-list.png)

## 2 · Place four notes

Tap the treble staff four times, roughly on the first four beats. Each tap puts a quarter note on the nearest line or space at the nearest quarter slot, and sounds it. Before the tap, a faint **ghost** shows exactly what will land and where.

![The ghost shows the note the tap will place, before it places it.](figure:ghost-note)

Wrong pitch? Put the pen down **on the notehead** and drag up or down — it moves by staff step, sounding each one, and the release commits it. Wrong value? Tap the note, then tap a different duration on the Notes rail: the note becomes that value, and that value is armed for the next tap too.

Wrong note altogether? Tap it and press **Delete** — a rest takes its place, because a bar always adds up.

## 3 · Write a chord, and a bass line

Tap a **different pitch at the same slot** as a note you already placed and the pitch joins the chord. Duration, dots and articulations belong to the chord as a whole; accidentals and ties belong to each pitch.

Now tap the lower staff. Nothing about the staves is modal — the pen decides which one it is writing on.

## 4 · Hear it

Press **play** on the Transport rail, or the space bar. Playback starts from the playhead at the piece's tempo, follows repeats and tempo marks, and the slider seeks. **Home** stops.

## 5 · Keep going

A new empty bar appears the moment the last one gets its first note, so you never run out of paper. Everything is **saved as you write** — there is no save button, and there never will be. With an account, the piece syncs to your other devices.

## 6 · Print it

**File ▾ → Export PDF** opens the export sheet: page size, staff size, margins, a header, and a live preview of the real page. **Save to device** or **Share…** gets you the file; **Save to Scores as PDF** drops it into your [Scores](app:scores) library, where a later send replaces it in place.

If you want bars 1–4 on the first row and bar 10 given extra room because it is dense, the **Layout** row in that sheet opens [the Layout editor](help:layout) and you arrange it on the paper itself.

## Then

Turn on [Gesture mode](help:gestures) — it is off until you ask for it — and the common moves stop needing a trip to Select.
