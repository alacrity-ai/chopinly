The editor is three things stacked: a **header**, however many **rails** you have asked for, and the **score** filling everything below them.

![The editor: header, rails, and the score below.](figure:anatomy-editor)

## The header

`‹` goes back to your compositions. The **title** in the middle is a button — tap it for the details sheet, where the title, composer and tags live. **File ▾** holds the exports. **Options ▾** is the control panel: what draws, gestures, favorites, which rails show, and help. See [the Options panel](help:options).

## The rails

Controls and Transport and Notes are shown by default; the other five are a tick away in Options. Each rail is **one lane, always** — when it does not fit it scrolls sideways under a finger, and it never wraps to a second line, so the score never loses room without you asking. [How the rails work](help:rails) explains the grammar they all share.

## The score

Systems wrap to the width of the screen and stack down the page, exactly as the PDF will page them. **What you edit is what you print** — there is no separate print view to be surprised by, only the [Layout editor](help:layout) if you want to arrange the paper deliberately.

`−` and `+` on the Controls rail zoom by re-drawing at a new staff size, not by scaling a picture, so the glyphs stay crisp at every step. Your zoom is remembered per device.

**The score holds still.** In Place and Select nothing pans, nothing zooms, and a palm resting on the glass does nothing at all. Panning is [its own mode](help:modes), and a mouse wheel always scrolls because a desk has no palm.

## The ghost

On a pen or a mouse, a faint copy of the note the next tap will place follows the tip — the right head, stem, dots and flag, at the pitch and slot it will land on. On a finger there is no hover, so the ghost appears when you touch down and the note lands where the ghost is when you lift.

## Bars

A piece starts with eight empty bars, and a **new one is appended the moment the last bar gets its first note**. Trailing empty bars are trimmed when you export and when you close, so a stray empty measure never reaches a PDF. To put a bar in the middle, or take one out, use insert and delete on [the Form rail](help:rail-form).

## Sound

A placed note sounds, a chord sounds together, and every step of a re-pitch drag sounds as it passes. Selecting is silent. The volume follows the app's own.

## Saving

Every committed edit saves itself, at once, on this device. Reopening restores the piece and your zoom. With an account it all syncs. See [saving and your library](help:saving).
