The first three buttons **arm a cursor**: tap the button, then tap where the change goes. The same change on the same place again removes it. `Esc` disarms.

## Key

Tap **key**, choose the signature from its `▾`, then tap the bar it starts on. It holds until the next change. Courtesy signatures at the change and cautionary accidentals after it follow standard practice, so you do not have to think about them.

## Time

Tap **time**, choose the signature, tap the bar. The bars from there are re-cut to the new metre by tick — notes move, sections stay with their bar numbers.

## Clef

Tap **clef**, choose it from the `▾`, then tap **a staff and a beat**. Unlike key and time, a clef change can land *inside* a bar, on any beat, because that is how a clef change is actually used.

## Articulations and ornaments

Staccato, accent, tenuto, fermata, trill, mordent, the lower mordent, turn — the **marks** group. These follow the ordinary rule: with a selection they stamp it, and the same mark again takes it off. They are placed above or below the head as the stems require.

Playback hears them — a fermata is held, a trill and a turn are played out.

## Slur

A slur joins *different* pitches, where a tie joins the same one twice.

Select a run and tap **slur**: it draws from the earliest selected note of a staff to the latest. Select one note and it slurs to the staff's next note, over rests. The same span again removes it. Slurs nest, may share a note as an end, are drawn on the head side, arch to clear what lies between, and split at a system break like a tie. Notes under a slur play legato.

## Glissando

Select two notes and tap **gliss**: a line runs from the first to the second.

## Rolled chords

**Arp** rolls the selected chord. Hold it for *rolled upward* and *rolled downward*, which add the arrow. Playback spreads the chord.

## Pickup

A partial opening or closing bar is on [the Form rail](help:rail-form), with the other bar operations.
