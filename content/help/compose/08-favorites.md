Favorites is a shortcut layer and nothing more. A favorite **is** a rail button — tapping it is tapping that button, with the same arming, the same behaviour on a selection, the same toast. It even shows the button's state, so a favorite of the quarter note lights up when the quarter is armed.

![The Favorites panel: six slots, eight pages, a grabber and an ×.](/img/help/favorites-panel.png)

## Summoning it

In **Place mode**, hold the pen — or a finger, in Touch mode — in one spot for **two seconds** without travelling. The panel opens with its grabber under your pointer, and the hold does not place anything. If it is already out, the hold **moves it to your hand**. One motion pulls the palette to where you are working.

![Hold two seconds in one spot.](figure:gesture-hold)

The hold only works in Place — Select and Pan are not for placing — and never on a notehead, because that is a grab. A Pen-mode finger is a palm and summons nothing.

You can also switch it on and off from [Options ▾](help:options) under *Input*, which shows it at wherever you last left it.

## Living with it

- The **grabber bar** at the top drags it anywhere, with pen or finger; it is clamped on screen and the place is remembered.
- The **×** hides it. Tapping the score does **not** — it stays out while you write, which is the point.
- Six slots in a 3 × 2 grid, and **eight pages** behind the ◀ *1 / 8* ▶ footer. Forty-eight slots, and the page you were on is remembered.

Page 1 arrives seeded with ♯, ♭, ♮, dot, tie and tuplet. Pages 2–8 start empty.

## Putting a button in a slot

1. Tap an **empty slot**. It starts listening — a pulsing outline, and the hint says so.
2. Tap **any rail button**. It is captured into the slot rather than fired.
3. Rows inside hold menus count: open the menu by hold or by its ▾, then tap *mp*, *ffff*, a quintuplet, the dashed *cresc.* — whatever you actually want.

Tapping the listening slot again, another slot, or the × cancels. The same button may sit in as many slots as you like.

**What cannot be a favorite**, with a toast saying so: anything on the header, Controls or Transport rails — File, Options, undo, redo, the modes, Pen ∣ Touch, Gesture, Favorites itself, the clipboard, zoom, play, tempo — a menu opener, and the *set* button of the text box, which needs the words you typed. Everything on the six palette rails is fair game.

## Clearing one

Hold a filled slot for a second and a half. It empties, with a tick and a *removed*. A plain tap fires it instead; a hold on an empty slot does nothing.

## What is remembered

All of it — the pages, the slots, the page you are on, the position, whether it is showing — **per device**. Favorites are deliberately not synced: a favorite names a rail button, and rails are a device's habit, like which ones you show.
