That page doesn't exist. The app lives at /app; the tools and the blog are below.

  • MetronomeA pendulum on the Web Audio clock; accents, subdivisions, tap tempo; survives a locked screen.
  • PianoA keyboard sized to the screen: chords, glides, sustain, one to four octaves.
  • TunerA chromatic tuner on the mic: cents dial, note name, hertz, A4 calibration.
  • Pitch pipeA sustained reference tone on any note in any octave; A4 calibration.
  • RecorderRecord takes from the clock; they stay on your device, attached to the goal; compare A and B.
  • Sight singingA melody on the staff, a count-in, then every note you sing is judged on pitch and rhythm.
  • Ear trainingHear a note or a chord, play it back — on the screen or on your real piano through the mic.
  • Practice logPress play, name the goal; minutes, notes, history and analytics follow — no forms.
