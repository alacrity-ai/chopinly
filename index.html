In the case

  • Metronome — a pendulum that keeps honest time on the Web Audio clock, with subdivisions and tempo markings, and keeps clicking behind a locked screen. Stamp a tempo onto the goal you're practicing.
  • Piano — a keyboard under your thumbs: chords, glides, a sustain pedal, one to four octaves, as big as the screen allows.
  • Recorder — record a take of what you're practicing, right from the clock. It stays on your device; listen back, star the keeper, compare this week against last.
  • Pitch pipe — any note, any octave, in tune.
  • Tuner — hear where you are.
  • Sight singing — graded melodies, sung back and judged; finished runs land in the logbook by themselves.
  • Ear training — hear a reference, hear a note or a chord, play it back on the keyboard or on your real piano through the mic; judged as you go.
  • Logbook — goals, the clock, notes, today, history.
  • Analytics — any range; by composer, work and type; time of day, weekdays, session length. Focus on one thing and every chart follows.
